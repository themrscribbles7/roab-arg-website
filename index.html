About ROAB

The Research of Abnormal Behaviour (ROAB) is a privately-funded research organization dedicated to the scientific investigation of unusual phenomena, anomalous behaviour, and unexplained environmental events. Our multidisciplinary team works to understand and document cases that fall outside conventional scientific explanation.

Founded by Professor Newton, ROAB operates several research facilities equipped with advanced monitoring and analysis systems. Our mission is to maintain rigorous scientific standards while investigating matters that traditional institutions find difficult to approach.

We investigate Anomaly cases with discretion and precision.
